# Gap 5 - Pricing Validation

**Prepared for:** VLU R3 Post-Grad Research  
**Date:** 2026-05-21  
**Output priority:** Decision-grade concise  
**Scope:** 24 R2 anchor programs + 6 Malaysia/Indonesia peers + 6 Vietnam competitors. Tuition is normalized to approximate USD for comparability.

## 1. Executive Decision

**Family Academy:** recommend **$15k / $20k / $35k**.

- $15k clears the "too cheap / low-quality" risk for Gen-2 family business buyers.
- $20k is the main anchor: high enough to signal selective executive education, still below RMIT Vietnam MBA and far below Singapore/Australia elite MBA pricing.
- $35k should be a **family package**, not just a premium learner seat: F1 + Gen-2 workshops, confidential family business diagnostic, advisory clinics, international module, and lifelong network access.
- Avoid $12k base for Family Academy except as limited founding scholarship because it risks positioning the product as another local MBA.
- Avoid $20k / $30k / $50k at launch because $50k is above the Vietnam foreign-degree ceiling without equivalent brand/accreditation proof yet.

**Healthcare MBA / Healthcare Leadership MBA:** recommend **$12k / $18k / $30k**.

- $12k is the self-pay base ceiling VLU can defend against UEH, FSB, FTU, NEU, and HCMUT-style healthcare management alternatives.
- $18k is plausible for employer-supported healthcare/pharma managers if the program includes live hospital/pharma capstone, healthcare faculty, and applied operations content.
- $30k should be sponsor/employer package only: cohort seats, executive coaching, hospital transformation project, and ASEAN/international module.
- Avoid $20k / $30k / $50k for Healthcare at launch; it would sit too close to or above foreign-degree reference points without equivalent credential power.

## 9. Launch Guardrails

| Guardrail | Rationale |
|---|---|
| Keep Family Academy sticker above $15k unless discount is explicitly a founding scholarship. | The product needs a premium, confidential, selective signal; too-low pricing invites generic MBA comparison. |
| Keep Healthcare self-pay entry at $12k-$15k until employer sponsorship is proven. | Healthcare buyers face existing MHA/local MBA alternatives and stronger ROI scrutiny. |
| Use scholarships as controlled discounting, not public price cuts. | Preserves premium anchor while allowing cohort formation. |
| Do not launch $50k tier in year 1. | Requires stronger proof: international accreditation/partner, named faculty, published outcomes, and sponsor demand. |
| Validate with buyer calls before public tuition lock. | Synthetic Van Westendorp is directional; final willingness-to-pay needs primary evidence. |

## 10. Final Recommendation

**Choose $15k / $20k / $35k for Family Academy** and position the $35k tier as a family transformation package, not a single-student upsell.

**Choose $12k / $18k / $30k for Healthcare MBA** and reserve the $30k tier for employer-sponsored or consortium-sponsored buyers.

This creates a clear two-product portfolio: Family Academy as VLU's premium wedge, Healthcare MBA as a disciplined vertical professional program with employer-funded upside.
