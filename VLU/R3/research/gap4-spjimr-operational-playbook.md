# Gap 4 - SPJIMR PGPFMB Operational Playbook for VLU Adaptation

Ngày research: 2026-05-21  
Benchmark: SPJIMR Post Graduate Programme in Family Managed Business (PGPFMB) + Centre for Family Business and Entrepreneurship (CFBE)  
Mục tiêu: bản concise, decision-grade để VLU chuyển hóa playbook PGPFMB thành Family Enterprise Academy tại Việt Nam.

## Executive Decision

PGPFMB không chỉ là một course về family business. Đây là một operating system gồm: tuyển chọn next-gen family business leaders, bắt buộc senior-generation mentor, học theo monthly contact, dùng chính family business làm business-as-lab, khai thác alumni network, và dùng CFBE research/report để tạo authority + lead flow.

Khuyến nghị cho VLU: copy logic vận hành, không copy nguyên lịch 6 ngày/tháng. VLU nên launch bản nhỏ, private và phù hợp Việt Nam: 12 tháng, 20-25 successors, mentor-required admissions, applied deliverables, Family Showcase, Vietnam Family Enterprise Outlook report, và trust-based distribution.

## Verified Facts

### 1. Positioning và Scale

- PGPFMB launched năm 1997; SPJIMR gọi đây là programme tiên phong tại India cho family-managed business. Public page ghi 37 batches và 4000+ alumni từ India, Nepal, Dubai, Hong Kong. Source: [SPJIMR PGPFMB overview](https://www.spjimr.org/course/post-graduate-programme-in-family-managed-business-pgpfmb/).
- CFBE gọi PGPFMB là flagship 15-month modular programme cho new entrants trong family businesses. Source: [CFBE education](https://www.spjimr.org/centre-of-practice/centre-for-family-business-and-entrepreneurship-cfbe/education/).
- FAQ ghi intake tối đa pre-Covid là up to 250 students/year. Batch 34 có 101 participants; Batch 33 có participants từ 11 states. Sources: [FAQ](https://www.spjimr.org/course/post-graduate-programme-in-family-managed-business-pgpfmb/faq/), [Batch 34](https://www.spjimr.org/newsroom/event/spjimr-welcomes-pgpfmb-batch-34/), [Batch 33](https://www.spjimr.org/newsroom/event/spjimr-welcomes-pgpfmb-batch-33/).

Decision implication: SPJIMR có scale nhờ 25+ năm trust và alumni density. VLU Cohort 1 không nên tối ưu scale; nên tối ưu cohort quality.

### 2. Calendar và Format

- Current curriculum page ghi programme 15 tháng: 12 tháng sessions liên tục, interim business plan presentation tháng 13, final presentation tháng 15. Source: [curriculum](https://www.spjimr.org/course/post-graduate-programme-in-family-managed-business-pgpfmb/curriculum/).
- Overview ghi modular programme với 6 ngày on-campus mỗi tháng trong 15 tháng; FAQ diễn giải là one week on Mumbai campus mỗi tháng. Sources: [overview](https://www.spjimr.org/course/post-graduate-programme-in-family-managed-business-pgpfmb/), [FAQ](https://www.spjimr.org/course/post-graduate-programme-in-family-managed-business-pgpfmb/faq/).
- Admissions rolling basis, 2 batches/year: April và September. Source: [admissions](https://www.spjimr.org/course/post-graduate-programme-in-family-managed-business-pgpfmb/admissions/).
- International immersion là một phần programme; public evidence có Babson, POLIMI, Esade. Sources: [international immersion](https://www.spjimr.org/course/post-graduate-programme-in-family-managed-business-pgpfmb/international-immersion/), [CFBE partners](https://www.spjimr.org/centre-of-practice/centre-for-family-business-and-entrepreneurship-cfbe/partners/).

Ghi chú: brochure/third-party listing cũ còn ghi 18 months hoặc fee cũ. Dùng current SPJIMR pages làm source-of-truth; dùng PDF cũ chỉ để hiểu historical design.

### 3. Curriculum và Assessment Evidence

- Current curriculum ghi 32 courses, 45 credits qua 3 phases: basic, advanced, electives. Source: [curriculum](https://www.spjimr.org/course/post-graduate-programme-in-family-managed-business-pgpfmb/curriculum/).
- Core elements xuất hiện trong official PDF/brochure: Family Dynamics I-III, Business Improvement Project, Business and Role Plan with B-Plan mentoring, Leadership Lab, Business & Society, finance, sales, strategy, legal, digital, brand, financial planning. Sources: [Course Architecture PDF](https://spjimr.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Course-Architecture-PGPFMB.pdf), [brochure PDF](https://www.spjimr.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/pgp-fmb-brochure.pdf).
- Course exam/grading policy không công khai. Public evidence chỉ xác nhận: admission aptitude test, business/future-plan presentation, interim business plan presentation, final presentation. Sources: [admissions](https://www.spjimr.org/course/post-graduate-programme-in-family-managed-business-pgpfmb/admissions/), [curriculum](https://www.spjimr.org/course/post-graduate-programme-in-family-managed-business-pgpfmb/curriculum/).

Decision implication: VLU không nên giả định một hidden exam system. Nên thiết kế assessment minh bạch dựa trên applied deliverables, capstone và mentor review.

### 4. Admissions và Family-Business Due Diligence

Eligibility verified:

- Undergraduate degree từ recognised university.
- Ít nhất second-generation member của family-managed business.
- Có significant stake trong business.
- Muốn carry forward family business legacy.
- Có senior-generation mentor từ gia đình. Source: [admissions](https://www.spjimr.org/course/post-graduate-programme-in-family-managed-business-pgpfmb/admissions/).

Admissions process verified:

- Online application.
- Online Aptitude Test.
- Interview.
- Assessment.
- Selection/Rejection.
- Candidate trình bày brief PowerPoint về business và future plans.
- Interview gồm participant và mentor, 1-2 rounds.
- Selection dựa trên 3 parameters: Candidate, Business, Mentor. Source: [admissions](https://www.spjimr.org/course/post-graduate-programme-in-family-managed-business-pgpfmb/admissions/).

Decision implication: admissions triad là moat chính. VLU phải tuyển cả candidate + family business + mentor, không chỉ tuyển student.

## Inferred Operating Model

### 1. Product Model

PGPFMB có vẻ vận hành theo applied learning loop:

1. Monthly contact week on campus.
2. Academic + clinical/practitioner teaching.
3. Peer discussion giữa family business successors.
4. Return to own business để apply.
5. Mentor engagement với senior generation.
6. Business Improvement Project / Business and Role Plan.
7. Interim và final presentations.

### 2. Admissions Model

Likely scoring logic:

| Dimension | What is screened |
|---|---|
| Candidate | readiness, aptitude, maturity, motivation |
| Business | real family business, scale, potential, usable test bed |
| Mentor | senior buy-in, time commitment, willingness to sponsor projects |
| Cohort fit | sector/geography diversity, peer value |

### 3. Economic Model

Likely revenue stack:

- Core revenue: tuition/programme fee.
- Separate/pass-through: hostel, travel, international immersion.
- Strategic revenue/upside: reports, events, partners, alumni ecosystem.
- Extension: vRddhi funding/advisory platform cho alumni/current participants. Source: [vRddhi](https://www.spjimr.org/faculty-research/research/centre-of-practice/centre-for-family-business-and-entrepreneurship-cfbe/vriddhi/).

### 4. Marketing Model

Likely funnel:

- Authority: SPJIMR brand + pioneer status + CFBE reports.
- Proof: alumni, cohort events, international immersion.
- Conversion: counselling + candidate/mentor interview.
- Retention/network: Family Fridays, Samagam, alumni access, ecosystem platforms.

## VLU Adaptation Blueprint

### 1. Launch Positioning

Programme: VLU Family Enterprise Academy - Next-Generation Leadership Program.

Target: gen-2/3 successors, 25-42 tuổi, từ Vietnamese family businesses có revenue VND 50B+ hoặc equivalent operating scale.

Non-negotiables:

- Candidate có real operating role hoặc succession path rõ.
- Senior family mentor required.
- Business được dùng làm learning lab.
- Application-only, không open enrollment.
- Confidentiality là product feature.

### 2. Format

Pilot recommendation:

- 12 tháng.
- 20-25 participants.
- Monthly 2-day weekend contact tại VLU.
- 2 intensives x 3 ngày: opening retreat và mid-program domestic immersion.
- Final Family Showcase Day với candidate + mentor.

Không copy 6 days/month ở Cohort 1. Format đó mạnh nhưng friction cao; VLU có thể thêm optional clinics sau khi có demand proof.

### 3. Curriculum

| Module | Applied output |
|---|---|
| Family Dynamics & Governance | Family Governance Map |
| Mentor-Successor Alignment | Mentor-Successor Charter |
| Strategy Refresh | Strategy Refresh Deck |
| Finance & Controls | Finance Control Dashboard |
| People & Professionalisation | Org Redesign Memo |
| Digital/AI Modernisation | Digital Roadmap |
| Legal, Tax & Ownership | Governance Risk Checklist |
| Capital & Family Office | Family Capital Blueprint |
| Succession Planning | Succession Plan v1 |
| Capstone | Family Enterprise Plan vFinal |

### 4. Assessment

Transparent applied assessment:

| Component | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Module deliverables | 35% |
| Capstone implementation | 25% |
| Mentor review | 15% |
| Peer contribution/confidentiality | 10% |
| Attendance/participation | 15% |

No high-stakes final exam. Minimum attendance: 80%.

### 5. Admissions Workflow

1. Qualification call.
2. Application: CV, family business profile, ownership/revenue band, succession challenge.
3. Mentor nomination letter.
4. NDA cho candidate và mentor.
5. Short executive judgment / family-business scenario screen.
6. 10-slide business and future-plan presentation.
7. Candidate interview.
8. Candidate + mentor interview.
9. Selection committee scoring: Candidate, Business, Mentor, Cohort Fit.
10. Registration deposit.

### 6. Staffing

Minimum viable team:

| Role | Why needed |
|---|---|
| Programme Director | trust, admissions, academic leadership |
| Programme Manager | calendar, logistics, student ops |
| BD/Admissions Lead | founder dinners, partner referrals, pipeline |
| Mentor in Residence | family dynamics, gen-1 engagement |
| Research Lead | Vietnam Family Enterprise Outlook |
| Clinical faculty pool | legal, tax, governance, finance, digital, HR, strategy |
| Advisory Board | credibility, referrals, curriculum relevance |

### 7. Pricing

Cohort 1 recommendation:

- Standard fee: VND 300m/student.
- Founder Circle early adopter: VND 250m cho first 10 accepted participants.
- Family Sponsor package: VND 420m gồm selected mentor/family sessions.

Payment waterfall:

- Application fee: VND 3m-5m, credited if enrolled.
- Registration deposit: 15%.
- First instalment before start: 45%.
- Second instalment Month 4: 40%.

Target pilot economics: 22 students x VND 300m = VND 6.6B gross programme revenue. Không dựa vào sponsorship ở Year 1; coi sponsorship là upside.

### 8. Marketing và Distribution

Primary channels:

- Private founder dinners.
- Partner referrals từ banks, Big 4, law firms, tax advisors, VCCI/HUBA, founder networks.
- 1:1 counselling với Programme Director.
- Vietnam Family Enterprise Outlook làm lead magnet.
- LinkedIn ABM cho named gen-2 profiles.
- Founder/successor testimonials chỉ dùng khi có consent.

Pilot funnel target:

| Stage | Target |
|---|---:|
| Named target family enterprises | 300 |
| Warm intros / event attendees | 120 |
| Qualified calls | 70 |
| Applications | 45 |
| Accepted | 25-28 |
| Enrolled | 20-25 |

### 9. Vietnam Family Enterprise Outlook

Launch report trước hoặc song song Cohort 1.

Year 1:

- Survey 150-250 family businesses.
- Topics: succession readiness, governance, digitalisation, professionalisation, capital, gen-1/gen-2 alignment.
- Dùng external research partner để tăng credibility.
- Publish aggregate findings only.
- Dùng report cho PR, private dinners, partner activation, admissions counselling.

### 10. Go/No-Go Criteria

Proceed only if:

- 18+ paid deposits.
- 25+ accepted candidates with mentor commitment.
- Programme Director + Programme Manager hired.
- NDA/confidentiality framework approved.
- 8+ clinical faculty confirmed.
- Advisory board seated.
- Research report plan approved.

Do not proceed if:

- Cohort quality yếu.
- Families không chịu mentor participation.
- Discounting trở thành sales lever chính.
- Confidentiality/legal framework chưa sẵn sàng.

## Bottom Line

SPJIMR's moat nằm ở integration: admissions triad, mentor buy-in, applied projects, alumni/community và research-led authority. VLU nên launch phiên bản Việt Nam nhỏ hơn, riêng tư hơn, minh bạch hơn: 20-25 successors, mentor-required admissions, applied deliverables, annual family-business report, và trust-first distribution engine.
