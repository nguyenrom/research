## 🔍 R2 Research scope (what was done)

**Question:** Study mô hình sau đại học (master & MBA) tương đồng VLU ở 4 markets (China, Aus, India, Sing). Criteria: target learners, proposition, key claims, proof points. Tổng hợp pattern chung — họ đang bán gì trên post-grad programs?

**Method:**
- 4 parallel research agents (one per market) — total runtime ~30 minutes
- 24 anchor programs (5–6 per market = 5 winners + 1 struggling/failure case)
- Specialized master's included (not just MBA)
- 6-layer analysis framework: Primary matrix / Selling heat-map / Admissions funnel / Course DNA / Whitespace / VLU-borrowable filter

**Output:**
- 1 master synthesis report (Vietnamese, 25 pp)
- 4 per-market raw research files (English, 12–22 pp each)
- 6 matrix tables + 10 priority-ranked steal patterns + 3 anti-patterns + 4 failure case studies

## 🎯 Top R2 findings (1-page summary)

### Steal-list (priority order)
1. **SPJIMR PGPFMB modular 6-day/month** → Family Academy direct copy (28-yr proven blueprint, 4,000+ alumni, NO placement service by design)
2. **ISB/IIM 1-year residential exec format** → default format for Healthcare MBA
3. **Great Lakes "1 founding faculty" brand transfer** → recruit 1 named Kellogg/Wharton/IMD anchor
4. **SPJIMR CFBE research-center publishing model** → "VLU Center for VN Family Business" + annual "State Report"
5. **ISB ELP / MBS-EY live-client consulting capstone** → signature institutional moat with named VN industry partner
6. **Specialized Master > MBA pattern** (Tsinghua MoF #3 / SMU MWM #1 / Macquarie Marketing #1) → don't force MBA to carry brand
7. **Lifetime alumni membership in tuition** (CKGSB / ISB / INSEAD) → embed by default
8. **Selective small-cohort intimacy** (IIMB EPGP 75 / Nanyang-Waseda 15) → cohort 20–80, never scale before credibility
9. **Buyer = parent/founder, not student** (SPJIMR B2B trá hình B2C) → Family Academy marketing speaks to gen-1
10. **Publish less than you can prove** (Ashoka discipline) → no placement claims until cohort 3+

### Don't-copy list
1. **Australian PR-pathway model** — VN không có PR cho thạc sĩ ngoại
2. **Hult recruiter-commission funnel** (98% via agents) — sụp đổ khi VN unis cải thiện English
3. **SP Jain Global jurisdiction-shopping** — Triple Crown accreditation hoặc không

### Failure DNA (6 cross-cutting patterns from 4 case studies)
1. Recruitment volume over admissions quality (accept >60% sustained = decay)
2. Marketing claims that can't survive adversarial scrutiny
3. Single revenue channel >40% = strategic capture
4. Defensive litigation amplifies original sin
5. Paper credentials without real moat
6. Founder-as-brand → founder-as-liability
