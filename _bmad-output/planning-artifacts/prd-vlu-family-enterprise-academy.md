## 1. Executive Summary

VLU Family Enterprise Academy là chương trình thạc sĩ ứng dụng 12 tháng, application-only, dành riêng cho **thế hệ kế nghiệp (gen-2/3)** của các doanh nghiệp gia đình Việt Nam có quy mô doanh thu ≥ 50 tỷ VND/năm. Đây là chương trình **đầu tiên loại hình này tại Việt Nam** — lấp khoảng trống thị trường mà không một trường đại học nào (Fulbright, RMIT, UEH, IMD-equivalent VN) đang phục vụ, dù theo PwC 2025 chỉ 14% doanh nghiệp gia đình VN có succession plan chính thức (thấp nhất Asia).

Mục tiêu chiến lược: đóng vai trò **wedge program** trong portfolio post-grad VLU — niche cực hẹp, depth cực cao, brand-defining. Pilot cohort 1 (22 học viên, 2027) đặt nền cho **brand "VLU = trường của thế hệ kế nghiệp Việt Nam"**, mở khóa Healthcare MBA (anchor) và Lifelong Membership (halo) trong cùng triple-stack strategy.

**Key targets 3 năm:**
- Cohort 1 (2027): 22 học viên, Y1 revenue $298,800
- Cohort 2 (2028): 25 học viên, Y2 revenue $510,900
- Cohort 3+4 (2029): 50 học viên, Y3 revenue $1,259,550
- 3-year cumulative net contribution: **$702,940 (34% margin)**
- Y0 capital required: **$97,500** (sau pre-sell offset)
- Break-even: cuối Y2

## 4. Target Personas (deep dive)

### 4.1 Primary Persona — "Anh Tuấn"

**Demographics:**
- 28–42 tuổi, 70% nam / 30% nữ
- Du học MBA/MSc tại Anh, Mỹ, Úc (50%) hoặc tốt nghiệp top VN (50%)
- Sống ở HCMC, Hà Nội, hoặc các tỉnh có DN gia đình lớn (Bình Dương, Đồng Nai, Bắc Ninh, Đà Nẵng)
- Tình trạng: đã/sắp/đang quay về kế nghiệp DN gia đình

**Family Business Profile:**
- Doanh thu DN gia đình: 50–500+ tỷ VND/năm
- Ngành đa dạng: F&B, retail, manufacturing, logistics, hospitality, healthcare, agriculture, real estate
- Tuổi DN: 15–35 năm (gen-1 founder vẫn active)
- Quy mô nhân sự: 50–500+ employees

**Psychographics:**
- Cảm thấy "in-between" 2 thế giới — modern MBA + tradition gia đình
- Lo lắng về di sản, áp lực gia đình, danger để fail
- Khao khát peer connection — "ai đó hiểu nỗi đau của mình"
- Sẵn sàng đầu tư cho năng lực và network có chất lượng

**Pain Points:**
1. "Bố không buông quyền" / "Bố không truyền đạt expectations rõ"
2. "Team cũ kháng cự đổi mới"
3. "Anh em / chú bác / cousins competing for stake"
4. "Không tách được tiền cá nhân và tiền công ty"
5. "Không có ai để discuss confidentially"

**Goals:**
- Giữ + scale DN gia đình thành công
- Modernize legacy biz (digital, AI, governance)
- Build personal credibility (not just "nepotism")
- Smooth succession không xung đột gia đình
- Long-term wealth + family unity

**Willingness to Pay:**
- Cá nhân: $5k–$15k
- Gia đình tài trợ: $10k–$30k
- Lifetime value: ~$42,500

### 4.2 Secondary Personas

#### "Gen-1 Founder" (Buyer/Influencer, không phải Student)
- 55–70 tuổi, đang plan succession
- Trả tiền cho con/cháu học
- Cần buy-in vào program credibility
- Tham gia 4 "Joint sessions" trong cohort
- **Marketing layer riêng cho persona này**

#### "Family CFO / In-house Advisor"
- 35–55 tuổi, không phải con cháu nhưng inner circle
- Đang manage tài chính DN gia đình
- Quan tâm Module 3 (Capital, Finance, Family Office)
- Có thể là alumni cho membership

#### "Aspiring Successor" (gen-2 chưa kế nghiệp)
- 25–32 tuổi, đang work corporate
- Sẽ về DN gia đình trong 1–3 năm
- Cần prepare for transition
- Có thể có scholarship pathway

#### "Single Family Office (SFO) Operator"
- COO/CFO của family office
- Quan tâm Module 3 + Module 4
- Có thể là Y3 spinoff Family Office Operator Program target

### 4.3 Anti-Personas (KHÔNG phải target)

- ❌ Fresh grads không có family business
- ❌ Hourly employees of family businesses
- ❌ Family business enthusiasts không thực sự sở hữu DN
- ❌ Media / press / consultants chỉ muốn "research insider"
- ❌ Học viên muốn academic PhD path (cho DBA Y3, không Family Academy Y1)

## 7. Learning Outcomes & Competency Framework

### 7.1 Program-level Learning Outcomes

By end of 12 months, học viên sẽ có khả năng:

**L1. Family Governance Mastery**
- Design + implement Family Constitution
- Set up Family Council với rõ ràng vai trò
- Resolve family conflicts qua structured process
- Communicate intergenerational expectations

**L2. Strategic Business Leadership**
- Lead strategic refresh cho legacy business
- Identify + drive digital/AI transformation initiatives
- Make data-informed M&A decisions
- Build modern management systems trong family business context

**L3. Financial Architecture**
- Separate personal vs business finances
- Design Family Office structure (single-family hoặc multi-family)
- Tax + wealth planning across generations
- Investment Policy Statement (IPS) creation

**L4. Succession Execution**
- Build formal succession plan với timeline
- Establish + chair board of advisors
- Mentor next-next-gen successors
- Execute leadership transition with minimal disruption

**L5. Peer Network & Lifelong Learning**
- Active participant trong lifelong alumni circle
- Mentor mentee pairing
- Continuous learning mindset post-program

## 8. Curriculum Requirements — Module by Module

### 8.1 Overall Curriculum Structure

- **Total duration:** 12 months
- **Total contact hours:** ~250 hours (60 in-class + 80 small group + 60 1:1 mentoring + 50 retreat/intensive)
- **Total student effort:** ~400–500 hours including self-study + capstone work
- **Credit equivalency:** Designed to map to 36 credits (application-oriented per Thông tư 23/2021)

### 8.2 Module 1 — Family Dynamics & Governance (Months 1–3)

**Goal:** Học viên establish foundation cho family governance + communication.

**Content (40 hours contact):**

| Week | Topic | Pedagogy | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Family system theory + archetypes | Lecture + case discussion | Personal family map |
| 2 | Communication patterns intergenerational | Role play + family dialogue exercises | Conversation script |
| 3 | Family Council design | Workshop + benchmark study | Council blueprint |
| 4 | Family Constitution components | Working session with lawyer specialist | Constitution v0 |
| 5 | Conflict resolution methodology | Mediator-led workshop | Mediation toolkit |
| 6 | International benchmarks (IMD/Kellogg readings) | Reading + discussion | Comparative essay |
| 7 | **Joint Session #1 with gen-1** | Facilitated dialogue | Shared expectations doc |
| 8 | Module 1 deliverable presentation | Peer + faculty review | **Family Constitution v1** |

**Faculty:**
- Lead Academic: Family business scholar (Visiting from IMD or equivalent)
- 2 Practitioners: Gen-2 success stories
- 1 Specialist: Family business lawyer

**Reading list (required):**
- Davis et al., "Family Business Governance: A Three-Circle Model"
- HBR cases: Mars Inc., S.C. Johnson, Hermès succession
- IMD case: "Leading the Family Business" curriculum extract
- Custom VN case: 1 anonymized VN family business (to be developed)

### 8.3 Module 2 — Strategic Continuity & Modernization (Months 4–6)

**Goal:** Học viên lead strategic refresh + digital/AI transformation cho legacy biz.

**Content (40 hours contact):**

| Week | Topic | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Strategy refresh framework | Strategy diagnostic |
| 2 | Digital transformation cho legacy biz | Digital readiness audit |
| 3 | **AI integration cho family biz** (new module per AI-era re-frame) | AI use case identification |
| 4 | M&A literacy (acquisition + divestment) | Deal screening framework |
| 5 | Innovation portfolios (core/adjacent/transformational) | Portfolio map |
| 6 | Customer-centricity + branding refresh | Brand refresh outline |
| 7 | Operating model redesign | Operating model v0 |
| 8 | Module 2 deliverable presentation | **Strategy Refresh Deck** |

**Faculty:**
- Lead Academic: Strategy professor
- 2 Practitioners: CEO of successfully transformed family biz
- 1 Specialist: Digital transformation consultant (e.g., from McKinsey VN)

### 8.4 Module 3 — Capital, Finance & Family Office (Months 7–9)

**Goal:** Học viên architect family financial structure for multi-generational wealth.

**Content (40 hours contact):**

| Week | Topic | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Family vs business finance separation | Financial architecture audit |
| 2 | Wealth structuring (holding companies, trusts) | Structure recommendation |
| 3 | Tax planning across generations | Tax strategy outline |
| 4 | Single Family Office (SFO) vs Multi-Family Office (MFO) | FO decision framework |
| 5 | Investment Policy Statement (IPS) design | IPS draft |
| 6 | Risk management + insurance | Risk register |
| 7 | Real estate + alternative investments | Asset allocation model |
| 8 | Module 3 deliverable presentation | **Family Office Blueprint + IPS** |

**Faculty:**
- Lead Academic: Finance professor
- 2 Practitioners: CFO of SFO + Family Office consultant
- 1 Specialist: Tax lawyer / wealth structuring advisor

### 8.5 Module 4 — Leadership Transition & Succession (Months 10–12)

**Goal:** Học viên produce executable Succession Plan + lead transition.

**Content (40 hours contact + 4-day retreat):**

| Week | Topic | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Succession framework (Davis-Hampton-Lansberg models) | Custom framework |
| 2 | Mentor methodology (gen-1 → gen-2 → gen-3) | Mentoring playbook |
| 3 | Board governance + non-family directors | Board composition design |
| 4 | **Joint Session #2 with gen-1** (mid-program reflection) | Updated expectations doc |
| 5 | Conflict resolution for succession disputes | Mediation case study |
| 6 | Communication around succession announcement | Communication plan |
| **4-day retreat** | Deep work on personal succession plan + peer review | Personal plan |
| 7 | **Joint Session #3 with gen-1** (succession framework presentation) | Family commitment |
| 8 | Module 4 deliverable presentation | **Formal Succession Plan + Timeline** |

**Faculty:**
- Lead Academic: Family business succession scholar
- 2 Practitioners: 1 successful transitioner + 1 mediator
- 1 Specialist: Organizational psychologist

### 8.6 Capstone — Live Succession Project + Family Showcase Day

**Throughout months 1–12, capped by Family Showcase Day (Month 13):**

Each student implements 1 milestone từ their Succession Plan vào DN gia đình thật. Có industry mentor (alumnus or external) + academic advisor.

**Capstone deliverables:**
1. Implementation plan (Month 6)
2. Mid-implementation report (Month 9)
3. Implementation evidence + impact analysis (Month 12)
4. **Family Showcase Day presentation** (Month 13): Student + gen-1 cùng present trước cohort + faculty + invited family members

**Family Showcase Day format:**
- 1 full day, semi-private (cohort + immediate family + 5–10 invited guests per student)
- Each student: 20 min presentation + 10 min Q&A
- Closing: cohort + family banquet
- This is the **emotional + brand centerpiece** of the program
