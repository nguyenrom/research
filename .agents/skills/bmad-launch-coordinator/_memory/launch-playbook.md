## Product Hunt Best Practices

### Optimal timing

| Day | Quality | Rationale |
|-----|---------|-----------|
| **Tuesday** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Best day — less competition than Monday, audience active |
| **Wednesday** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Strong alternative |
| **Thursday** | ⭐⭐⭐ | Acceptable |
| **Monday** | ⭐⭐ | Heavy competition |
| **Friday** | ⭐ | Distracted audience, weekend incoming |
| **Weekend** | ❌ | Avoid — low engagement |

**Optimal time:** 00:01 PST (allows the listing to ride a full day).

### Winning PH page structure

```
HEADLINE: [Action verb] + [Primary benefit] + [For whom]
- Max 60 characters
- No technical jargon
- Focus on the outcome, not the feature

TAGLINE: [Problem solved] in [time / way]
- Max 100 characters
- Specific and measurable when possible

DESCRIPTION:
- Paragraph 1: The problem (2–3 sentences)
- Paragraph 2: The solution (2–3 sentences)
- Paragraph 3: Why now / differentiation (2–3 sentences)
- Final CTA

IMAGES:
- Image 1: Hero shot (product in action)
- Image 2: Before / After or problem / solution
- Image 3: Key features
- Image 4: Social proof or results
- GIF: 10–15 second demo
```

### Maker-comment template

```
Hey Product Hunt! 👋

[1 sentence — who you are and your background]

[2–3 sentences — why you built this; the personal problem you were solving]

[1–2 sentences — what makes the solution unique]

[1 sentence — the kind of feedback you'd love to get]

Would love to hear your thoughts! 🙏

[First name]
```

## Hunter Outreach Templates

### Template 1 — Direct & personal
```
Subject: Quick question about hunting [Product]

Hi [Hunter Name],

I noticed you hunted [Similar Product] a few months ago — it was a great launch!

I'm launching [Product] on [Date] — it's [one-liner description].

Would you be interested in hunting it? Happy to give you early access to try it out first.

[First name]
```

### Template 2 — Value-first
```
Subject: [Product] launch — thought of you

Hey [Hunter Name],

Been following your hunts — you clearly have great taste in [Category] tools.

I'm building [Product] which [unique value prop]. We already have [traction metric].

I think your audience would love it. Would you be open to hunting us on [Date]?

Happy to share a demo or early access.

Best,
[First name]
```

### Template 3 — Follow-up
```
Subject: Re: [Previous Subject]

Hey [Hunter Name],

Just following up on my previous email about [Product].

Quick update: [New traction or feature]

Still hoping you might be interested in hunting us. No pressure if not — just let me know either way!

Thanks,
[First name]
```

### Recommended cadence
- **J-21:** First email.
- **J-14:** Follow-up if no reply.
- **J-10:** Final follow-up with urgency cue.
- **J-7:** Confirm with hunter or trigger plan B (self-hunt).

## Master Checklist Template

### T-14 (2 weeks before)
- [ ] Lock the launch date.
- [ ] Contact 5–10 potential hunters.
- [ ] Build the asset checklist.
- [ ] Draft the first PH page.
- [ ] Identify communities to mobilize.

### T-7 (1 week before)
- [ ] Confirm hunter or commit to self-hunt.
- [ ] Finalize all visuals (screenshots, GIF).
- [ ] Write the maker comment.
- [ ] Prepare social posts.
- [ ] Create email templates for early supporters.
- [ ] Test the product end-to-end.

### T-3 (3 days before)
- [ ] Submit the PH draft.
- [ ] Send a preview to early supporters.
- [ ] Schedule social posts.
- [ ] Prepare the directory submission list.
- [ ] Brief the team on the J-Day plan.

### T-1 (day before)
- [ ] Final review of the PH page.
- [ ] Test every link.
- [ ] Set up J-Day environment (coffee, screens, comms).
- [ ] Notify supporters of timing.
- [ ] Sleep early!

### J-Day
- [ ] 00:01 PST: launch goes live.
- [ ] Post the maker comment immediately.
- [ ] Notify your closest community first.
- [ ] Post on social channels.
- [ ] Reply to EVERY comment.
- [ ] Regular ranking updates internally.
- [ ] Sustained engagement all day.

### J+1 to J+7
- [ ] Thank every supporter.
- [ ] Analyze metrics.
- [ ] Document learnings.
- [ ] Submit to directories.
- [ ] Reach out to media / blogs.
- [ ] Plan the next momentum push.
