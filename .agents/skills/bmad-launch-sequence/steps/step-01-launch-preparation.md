# Step 01 — Launch Preparation (J-14 to J-7)

**Phase:** 1
**Lead agent:** bmad-launch-coordinator
**Timebox:** J-14 → J-7

## Steps

1. **Launch Brief** (bmad-launch-coordinator) — *J-14* — Define the product/feature being launched, launch date and time, target audience segments, key messaging, success metrics, and budget allocation. → output: `launch-brief.md`
2. **Asset Inventory** (bmad-launch-coordinator) — *J-14* — Identify required assets: landing pages, email sequences, social content, press release, demo/tutorial videos, sales enablement. → output: `asset-checklist.md`
3. **Launch Content Creation** (bmad-content-architect) — *J-14 to J-10* — Create core content: landing-page copy, blog announcement, 5–7 email sequence, FAQ document. → output: `launch-content.md`
4. **Social Launch Content** (bmad-social-media-strategist) — *J-10 to J-7* — Create teaser posts (J-7 to J-1), launch-day posts, post-launch follow-up, and engagement templates. → output: `social-launch-content.md`
5. **SEO Preparation** (bmad-seo-strategist) — *J-10* — Optimize landing-page SEO, blog post optimization, schema markup, and internal linking plan. → output: `seo-launch-prep.md`

## References for this step

- `references/directory-tracker-protocol.md` — Begin populating Tier 0-3 directory targets and 3-week Product Hunt timeline if PH is part of the launch.

## Exit criteria

- Launch brief approved by user.
- All 5 outputs saved under `{marketing_artifacts}/`.
- No blocker on technical readiness (proceed to Step 02).
