## Landing Page Structure (PASO)

```markdown
# Landing Page: [PRODUCT / OFFER]

## 1. PROBLEM (Hero Section)
**Headline:** [Primary pain point — max 10 words]
**Subheadline:** [Amplify the problem]
**Visual:** [Suggested image / video]

## 2. AGITATE (Problem Section)
- Consequence 1 of the problem
- Consequence 2 of the problem
- Consequence 3 of the problem
"If you don't solve this..."

## 3. SOLUTION (Product Section)
**Headline:** [How [Product] solves the problem]
- Benefit 1 → Feature
- Benefit 2 → Feature
- Benefit 3 → Feature

## 4. PROOF (Social Proof)
- Testimonial 1 (concrete result)
- Testimonial 2 (transformation story)
- Customer logos / Headline numbers

## 5. OFFER (CTA Section)
**Headline:** [Urgency or exclusivity hook]
**CTA Button:** [Action + Benefit]
**Risk Reversal:** [Money-back guarantee or no-card line]
```

## Pillar Page Structure

```markdown
# Pillar: [CENTRAL TOPIC]

## Meta
- **Length:** 3,000–5,000 words
- **Audience:** [Beginner → expert]
- **Goal:** Become THE reference resource on this topic

## Structure

### Introduction (300 words)
- Hook + context
- Why this topic matters
- What the reader will learn
- Table of contents

### Section 1: [Fundamentals] (500–800 words)
- Clear definition
- Why it matters
- → Link to Cluster Article 1

### Section 2: [How to do it] (800–1,000 words)
- Step-by-step process
- Concrete examples
- → Link to Cluster Article 2
- → Link to Cluster Article 3

### Section 3: [Best practices] (500–800 words)
- Do's and don'ts
- Common mistakes
- → Link to Cluster Article 4

### Section 4: [Tools / Resources] (400–600 words)
- Recommended tool list
- Downloadable templates
- → Link to Cluster Article 5

### Section 5: [Case studies] (500–800 words)
- Real example 1
- Real example 2
- → Link to Cluster Article 6

### Conclusion + CTA (200 words)
- Recap of key points
- Next steps
- Primary CTA

## Cluster Articles to Create
1. [Topic 1] — 1,500 words
2. [Topic 2] — 1,500 words
3. [Topic 3] — 1,500 words
4. [Topic 4] — 1,500 words
5. [Topic 5] — 1,500 words
6. [Topic 6] — 1,500 words
```

## Repurposing Matrix

| Source | Twitter Thread | LinkedIn Post | Newsletter | Short Video | Carousel |
|--------|----------------|---------------|------------|-------------|----------|
| Blog article | 7–10 tweets, key points | 1 main insight + story | Summary + link | 60s on THE main tip | 5–7 slides of takeaways |
| Pillar page | Series of 3 threads | Series of 5 posts | 3-email mini-course | Series of 3 shorts | Series of 3 carousels |
| Landing page | Thread on the problem | Post on the transformation | Sales email | Customer testimonial video | Before / After |

## Headline Formulas

### For blog articles
- "How to [Achieve X] Without [Pain Point Y]"
- "[Number] [Adjective] Ways to [Desired Outcome]"
- "The Complete Guide to [Topic] in [Year]"
- "Why [Common Belief] Is Wrong (And What to Do Instead)"
- "[Topic]: Everything You Need to Know"

### For landing pages
- "[Achieve X] in [Timeframe] — Guaranteed"
- "Stop [Pain Point]. Start [Desired State]."
- "The [Adjective] Way to [Outcome] That [Target Audience] Love"
- "[Number]+ [Target Audience] Trust [Product] to [Benefit]"

### For CTAs
- "Get [Benefit] Now"
- "Start [Doing X] Free"
- "Yes, I Want [Outcome]"
- "Show Me How"
- "Claim Your [Offer]"
