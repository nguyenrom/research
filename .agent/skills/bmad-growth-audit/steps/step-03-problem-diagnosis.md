# Step 03 — Problem Diagnosis (SWOT)

**Phase:** 3
**Lead agent:** bmad-growth-analyst

## Steps

1. **Identify Strengths** (bmad-growth-analyst) — Document high-performing channels, successful content types, effective campaigns, strong conversion points, and competitive advantages. → output: `strengths.md`
2. **Identify Weaknesses** (bmad-growth-analyst) — Document underperforming channels, conversion blockers, content gaps, technical issues, and resource inefficiencies. → output: `weaknesses.md`
3. **Identify Opportunities** (bmad-growth-analyst) — Document untapped channels, audience segments, content opportunities, conversion optimizations, and quick wins. → output: `opportunities.md`
4. **Identify Threats** (bmad-growth-analyst) — Document competitive threats, platform risks, dependency issues, market changes, and sustainability concerns. → output: `threats.md`

## Quality bar

- Every entry must reference a specific data point from Step 01 or Step 02. SWOT bullets without evidence are filler.
- Strengths must be defensible (durable advantage, not one-off luck).
- Threats must include at least one platform-dependency risk and one competitive risk.

## Exit criteria

- 4 SWOT outputs produced.
- Every bullet sourced to a data artifact from Steps 01-02.
