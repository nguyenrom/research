Executive view · outcomes for AmCham

What this platform and its AI workflows mean for the chamber's leadership.

The decision in one line: one platform to retain members, grow non-dues revenue and run a lean team, with AmCham owning 100% of its data and code.

For the President / Executive Director

  • Retain members. The AI agent flags at-risk members early (Workflow 1) so renewals are saved, not lost to silence.
  • Grow non-dues revenue. Events, sponsorship and upsell briefings (Workflow 2) live in one place; finance reconciles with QuickBooks.
  • Run lean. Automation lets a small team do more; one platform replaces ChamberMaster, Constant Contact and spreadsheets.
  • Own it, lower the risk. 100% source-code and data ownership, PDPA by design, no per-member SaaS fees, no vendor lock-in.

For Marketing & Membership

  • Engage members. Unified, brand-consistent communications across both chapters (replaces Constant Contact).
  • Always-on follow-up. Event recaps, thank-yous and re-engagement are drafted automatically (Workflows 3 & 1); staff simply approve.
  • Better member experience. Self-service portal, searchable directory and frictionless event registration.
  • Decide with data. One member view plus dashboards for renewal, attendance and engagement.
