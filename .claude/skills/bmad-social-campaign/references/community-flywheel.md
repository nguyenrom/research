# Community Flywheel Reference

> Reference loaded during `bmad-social-campaign` and `bmad-growth-loops-sprint` for community-marketing branches. Owner: `bmad-social-media-strategist` and `bmad-growth-loop-designer` jointly.

## The Community Flywheel

```
       Identity
         │
         ▼
   First 100 set culture ──► Rituals create rhythm
         │                       │
         ▼                       ▼
    Lurkers participate ◄─── Members invite friends
         │                       ▲
         ▼                       │
     Advocates emerge ──────────-┘
```

Each rotation compounds: identity → first members → rituals → participation → invites → more identity-aligned members.

## Rule: identity > product

A community is built around **who members are**, not what your product does. The fastest-dying communities are "users of <product>" channels. Strong communities have an identity statement: "We are X who care about Y."

Examples:
- ❌ "Notion users" — community will fragment into use-case silos
- ✅ "Solo founders building knowledge businesses" — identity drives loyalty, product use is downstream

## Platform Selection Table

| Platform | Strengths | Weaknesses | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discord | Real-time, voice, low friction, threading | Discoverability poor, archival weak | Practitioners + creators, age 18-35 |
| Slack | Pro tone, integrations, threads | Costs scale with size, no public discovery | B2B operators, enterprise |
| Circle | Built for community, gating, courses | Lower engagement than chat | Paid communities, programs |
| Reddit | Built-in discovery, no maintenance | No control, hostile to brands | Top-of-funnel, awareness |
| Facebook Groups | Massive reach, mature audiences | Algorithm noisy, minimum viable trust | Consumer / B2C, age 30+ |
| Discourse | Long-form, search, persistent | Less real-time, requires moderation | Open-source projects, dev tooling |
| Twitter/X reply | No infrastructure | No persistence, depends on host's account | Founder-led, audience already on X |

## First-100 Protocol (sets culture forever)

The first 100 members irrevocably define the community's tone, taste, and aesthetic. Treat them as co-founders.

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 0 | Hand-curate the first 25. DM each one personally. Tell them why you chose them. |
| 0-7 | Onboard each by hand. Welcome them in-channel by name. Ask them to introduce themselves and their work. |
| 7-30 | Run 2-3 founder-led rituals (AMAs, build-with-me, weekly digest). They participate; you reinforce. |
| 30-60 | Identify 5-10 emerging power members. Give them a role (mod, ambassador, channel host). |
| 60-90 | Open invites publicly. Power members onboard new members. You step back from welcome duty. |

## Health Metrics

Track monthly:

| Metric | Healthy threshold | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| DAU / MAU | > 20% | Stickiness (vs lurker-only) |
| 7-day post rate from new members | > 30% | First-week activation |
| Replies per post | > 1.5 | Conversation, not broadcast |
| Top-10-poster share of total posts | < 60% | Distributed contribution (vs founder-only show) |
| 30-day member retention | > 70% | Identity match |
| Public mentions per week | trending up | Word-of-mouth working |

## Rituals (rhythm)

A community without rituals decays. Establish 2-4 named, recurring rituals:

- **Weekly digest**: Top conversations of the week, 1 page, send to all members on Friday
- **Open mic**: Specific channel/time where members share what they're working on, twice a week
- **AMA cadence**: 1 founder/customer/expert per month, same day-of-month
- **Member spotlight**: 1 member per week, profile them in-channel

## Anti-Spam Guardrail

Communities die from outsider self-promotion. Establish from day 1:

- No links in first-message-after-joining
- Self-promotion limited to a single dedicated channel + one designated day
- Mods enforce 90/10 rule
- Discord/Slack bot to flag link-heavy posts from new members

## Mod Relationships (when going public)

When public, your mods (paid or volunteer) make or break the community. Spend time on:

- Clear written norms doc
- Monthly mod office hours
- Mod-only Slack/channel for discussing edge cases
- Recognition (titles, swag, paid contracts for the most active)
