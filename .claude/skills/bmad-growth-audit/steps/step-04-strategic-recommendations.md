# Step 04 — Strategic Recommendations

**Phase:** 4
**Lead agent:** bmad-growth-analyst

## Steps

1. **Quick Wins** (bmad-growth-analyst) — Identify low-effort, high-impact actions, immediate optimizations, easy fixes, and Week-1 priorities. → output: `quick-wins.md`
2. **Strategic Initiatives** (bmad-growth-analyst) — Define 90-day priorities, resource requirements, expected impact, and success metrics. → output: `strategic-initiatives.md`
3. **Resource Recommendations** (bmad-growth-analyst) — Recommend channel budget allocation, team focus areas, tool recommendations, and outsourcing suggestions. → output: `resource-recommendations.md`
4. **KPI Framework** (bmad-growth-analyst) — Propose north-star metric, leading indicators, lagging indicators, tracking cadence, and reporting structure. → output: `kpi-framework.md`

## Recommendation discipline

- Quick wins = ICE > 7, ship within 5 business days. If it can't ship in a week, it isn't a quick win.
- Strategic initiatives must include explicit OPPORTUNITY COST: "doing X means we cannot do Y".
- KPI framework must distinguish input (controllable) from output (lagging) metrics.

## Exit criteria

- 4 outputs produced.
- Each recommendation tied to a SWOT entry from Step 03.
- KPI framework has at least 1 north-star metric, 3-5 input metrics, 2-3 guardrails.
