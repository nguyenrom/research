# Step 02 — Deep Analysis

**Phase:** 2
**Lead agent:** bmad-growth-analyst

## Steps

1. **Funnel Analysis** (bmad-growth-analyst) — Stage-by-stage conversion, drop-off points, attribution modeling, customer journey mapping, and bottleneck identification. → output: `funnel-analysis.md`
2. **Channel ROI Analysis** (bmad-growth-analyst) — Cost per acquisition by channel, revenue attribution, channel efficiency, investment vs. return, and channel synergies. → output: `channel-roi.md`
3. **Cohort Analysis** (bmad-growth-analyst) — Acquisition cohorts, retention curves, LTV by cohort, behavior patterns, and predictive insights. → output: `cohort-analysis.md`
4. **Competitive Benchmarking** (bmad-growth-analyst) — Traffic, social following, content output, SEO position comparison, and market-share estimates. → output: `competitive-benchmarking.md`

## Decision rules

- **Funnel binding constraint**: identify THE single largest absolute drop-off; resist the temptation to fix many leaks. Theory of Constraints applies.
- **Channel ROI honesty**: discount last-touch attribution unless you have multi-touch evidence. Mark each ROI number with confidence (high/med/low).
- **Cohort retention curve shape**: flat = good, declining = leak, accelerating decline = serious. Mark which cohorts trigger churn-prevention sprint.

## Exit criteria

- 4 outputs produced.
- Funnel binding constraint clearly named with quantified loss.
- ROI numbers tagged by confidence.
